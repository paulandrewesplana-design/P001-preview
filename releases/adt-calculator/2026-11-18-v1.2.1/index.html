03 / CALCULATION DETAILS

How ADT is calculated

Theo source: Nevada Gaming Commission Regulation 25.010(6)Game Theo = Average Bet × Hours/Day × Gaming Days × Decisions/Hour × House AdvantageEstimated ADT = Sum of Game Theo ÷ Days Counted for ADT

Nevada regulation defines theoretical earning potential, not a universal ADT day-count rule. The ADT divisor and default game-speed / house-edge values are estimating assumptions, not officially certified ratings. Counted days may differ from room nights or game-day totals.

Rating assumptions: Default house edges and hands per hour are illustrative estimates drawn from a historical casino player-rating example, not Nevada-mandated figures or verified property-specific values. See industry reference.
