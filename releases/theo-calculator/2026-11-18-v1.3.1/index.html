03 / CALCULATION DETAILS

How Theo is calculated

Nevada Gaming Commission Regulation 25.010(6) — Theoretical earning potentialTheo = Average Bet × Hours Played × Decisions per Hour × House Advantage

For multi-day play, the daily result is multiplied by gaming days and game results are summed. Nevada regulation defines the formula, but the default house-edge percentages and game-speed assumptions in this calculator are independent planning estimates, not values certified by the regulator or a casino property.
